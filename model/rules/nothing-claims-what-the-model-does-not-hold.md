# Nothing claims what the model does not hold

> No specification, page or statement made for us claims a fact or a number about us or our work that the model does not hold; a fact about code, such as a command's flags, is taken from the repository that holds the code.

## Why

The model is where a fact about GuestGraph is mastered, entered only from prose we have already published and reviewed; a claim made anywhere else has neither, and a reader cannot tell it from one that does. Where a fact is missing, it is added to the model first, and then it can be said.
