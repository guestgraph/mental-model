# A commit keeps the author it was made with

> A commit reaches the default branch under the author and address it was made with.

## Why

Our history tells a contributor from a seat and a seat from the Owner by the author alone: on September 28, 2026 we decided that an agent's commit is authored by the seat it held, and a contributor commits under their own name. A squash re-authors a commit to whoever pressed the button, and a wrong identity lands looking correct. Merging with a merge commit, never a squash, is this rule, and so is never asking a contributor to commit as anyone but themselves.
