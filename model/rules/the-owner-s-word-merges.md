# The Owner's word merges

> A merge, a tag or a release happens only on the Owner's word, given in the conversation where it is carried out, and goes no further than that word covers.

## Why

A change binds once it is merged, tagged or released, and whether it is true is the one thing an agent cannot supply; on September 29, 2026 we decided that agents write our model and a person approves every change to it. A word given in another conversation, or for another change, may rest on a state that has since moved, so it does not carry over, and an agent that asks for the word may carry out the step itself once it is given.
