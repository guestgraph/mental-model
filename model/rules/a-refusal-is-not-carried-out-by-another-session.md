# A refusal is not carried out by another session

> An action refused in one session, by the Owner or by its permission settings, is not carried out by another session, whatever the first session asks.

## Why

A refusal is the Owner's decision. A session that does the refused thing for another bypasses it, and the history shows a change nobody approved.
