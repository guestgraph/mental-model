# Whether a hotel would pay to know its guests is answered

> Whether the scattered-guest problem is real enough in a hotel's operation that someone would pay to have it solved is settled, in either direction, by hotels rather than by me.

## What it makes true

Today the engine is built, one real system is connected, nothing runs in production and there are no customers; the billing page says what the hosted service would count and that there is nothing to buy yet. That is a foundation and an open question. When this holds, the question has been put to people who run hotels and answered by what they did: either a hotel paid for the hosted service, or it is written down that nobody would, and the pages stop implying the question is live. A no settles it as completely as a yes, and a no is worth more to me than a polite yes.

What falls outside it: how many hotels, and at what price. One hotel paying answers the question; a larger one does not answer it better. Also outside is whether the open-source core is worth using, which a hotel running it for itself answers without paying anyone.
