# Contributor

> Someone outside the project who offers a change to one of our repositories, under the same rules a change from inside is held to.

## What it takes

The repository, its conventions, its constitution and its specifications, all of which are published; and the rules a change is held to, named by us where a contributor could not be expected to know them.

## What it produces

A pull request against the default branch, committed under their own address, saying what is now true that was not before.

## What it never does

- Never commits under an address that is not theirs, and is never asked to.
- Never merges, tags or releases.
