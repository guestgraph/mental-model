# id.blust.ch

> Signs readers in to the chat on guestgraph.io with ZITADEL, the identity service blust.ch runs at https://id.blust.ch in its Google Cloud project blust-ch-mcp in the Zurich region for the chats on blust.ch, companygraph.io and guestgraph.io, where accounts are by invitation only, and issues the token from which the chat reads the models a reader may choose.
