# Connector

> Software that brings one source system's data into the guest graph, run beside the engine.

## What it means

A product of this kind is opened by whoever operates the engine for a hotel that keeps its stays in that source system; it holds no data of its own and serves nothing but the engine. The Apaleo Connector is this kind. The service it feeds is Service.
